No interior optimum. Augmentation is simply harmful.
No dose beats “off” on both seeds with non-overlapping intervals. The dose knob was built to allow an interior answer and the data declined to give one — which is why the criterion was written that way rather than as a search for a better setting.

The curve is not monotone — and that is the interesting part

Secondary criterion: not met. EER rises to a peak at dose 0.50 and then improves at 1.0, on both seeds. Dose 1.0 beats dose 0.50 with non-overlapping intervals on both seeds (45.09 < 45.30 and 38.87 < 39.30), so this is a real effect, not noise.

seed 42: 0.0:33.979 -> 0.25:37.796 -> 0.5:45.828 -> 1.0:44.541

seed 43: 0.0:35.921 -> 0.25:35.774 -> 0.5:39.861 -> 1.0:38.304

Plausible reading: at full strength the model can still learn an invariance to the distortions; at half strength it gets a noisier, less consistent input distribution and pays the cost without gaining the invariance. Either commit to the augmentation or don't do it — the middle is the worst place to be.

Conclusion

Remove or permanently disable the codec/telephony augmenter. Do not tune it.
docs/20 could only say “this on/off setting is worse on”. docs/21 says the entire dose axis is worthless — including the points nobody had tried. That is a stronger and more actionable finding, and it is the reason this experiment was worth ~14 h of GPU.

Not re-run to chase a different answer (ground rule 5). Published as measured.
